# Product review rubric

Review Deep Fryer as a local Windows meme image editor for a first-time user and a returning creator. Review the product that exists at the named revision. Do not infer completed behavior from plans or marketing.

Score each category from 0 to 10. Use half points when useful. The overall score is the arithmetic mean of the four categories. Judge independently. Do not coordinate scores with other reviewers. An outstanding defect in data safety or a broken core workflow caps the overall score at 6.

- Usefulness. Opening an image, making a compelling edit, comparing it, reusing it, and sharing the result form a coherent workflow. Added features earn credit for concrete user value.
- Interface. Visual hierarchy, spacing, typography, discoverability, feedback, keyboard access, contrast, empty states, and small-window behavior support the workflow.
- Reliability. Real desktop journeys support the claims. Data safety, error handling, repeatability, offline operation, and output fidelity have appropriate evidence.
- GitHub presentation. A visitor can understand the app, see real results, download it, learn its limitations, report a problem, and contribute. Claims, links, licensing, and installation guidance are accurate.

Score anchors apply equally to every category.

- 0 to 2. Missing or unusable.
- 3 to 4. Major gaps prevent normal use.
- 5 to 6. Functional with obvious friction or missing evidence.
- 7 to 8. Strong and useful with material remaining weaknesses.
- 9. Polished, coherent, well supported, and ready to recommend for the stated purpose. Remaining issues are minor.
- 10. Exceptional within the stated scope, with no substantive improvement identified.

Return the revision, evidence inspected, four scores, computed overall score, specific weaknesses with file or artifact references, and the highest-value next improvement. Distinguish source review, built Electron, packaged application, hosted CI, and published release. Say when evidence is missing. Do not inflate a score because improvements were attempted. Preserve independent judgment across rounds.
